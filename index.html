About us

Yireh Ministry was founded by Pastor Srikanth katamala and Bro Srimanth katamala whose calling was confirmed through dreams, prophetic words, and spiritual encounters. The vision? To build a Christ-centered ministry where broken hearts are healed, souls are saved, and worship rises with purity and power.

From viral worship songs to life-changing gatherings in India and USA, this is the story of a simple “Yes” to God that became a global testimony.
