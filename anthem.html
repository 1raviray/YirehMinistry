Guidelines

Before you submit

Who can apply?

Any believer with a heart to worship — no prior release history required.

Submission rules

Only original work. Songs must be Scripture-rooted and Christ-centered.

Copyright notice

You retain authorship. Yireh Ministry receives a limited license to review and, if selected, produce and distribute the song.

What we review

We prayerfully consider lyrics, melody, and theological soundness before selection.

FAQ

Frequently asked

How long until I hear back?

We aim to respond within 4-6 weeks. Selected songs may take longer.

Can I submit more than one song?

Yes — please submit each song separately.

Do I need studio-quality audio?

No. A clear voice memo is enough for review.

Will I be credited?

Yes — every songwriter is properly credited.
