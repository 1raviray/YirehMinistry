THE Vision

A movement, not just a ministry

The name "Yireh" is drawn from Genesis 22:8 — "God Himself will provide the lamb for the burnt offering." This verse reminds us that God is our ultimate provider, the one who meets every need beyond what we can imagine. Our vision is to create a community where people can experience God's presence, find hope in their struggles, and grow in faith — through worship, teaching, outreach, and encouragement.

God has already opened doors to lead worship and minister in churches across India, Dubai, America, Australia, and the UK — confirmation that the calling on this ministry is real and expanding.

A word from Srimanth

"I gave God my excuses, but He gave me His Spirit."

Hello everyone! I am Srimanth Katamala, pakka Hyderabadi. From a young age, God planted a prophetic word over my life. At a youth meeting, a dear sister in Christ prophesied: "You have a beautiful voice, and one day you will worship before thousands. Learn to play the guitar, for God will use you powerfully." I was just a 9th grader and couldn't fully understand it — but those words stayed alive in my heart.

Like many, I had worldly ambitions — a great job, a good income, a comfortable life. I even entertained dreams of running a secular live band. But my mom and elder brother kept me grounded in faith. Her constant reminder: "Stay connected to the Lord, keep walking in His ways, and He will lift you up to places that will shock and surprise." I didn't have the best mic, gear, or platform. But the Lord told me: my Spirit is enough for you. From 500 followers to thousands touched across nations — this was never about fame. It's about faithfulness to the call.

About Pastor Srikanth Katamala

My vision for Yireh Ministry is to build not just a ministry, but a movement — one that reaches the lost, ministers to the broken-hearted, and empowers believers to walk boldly in their calling.This is a divine assignment to provide spiritual nourishment and practical hope in today's challenging world. Today, I surrender fully to God's plan — to worship Him, to lead others closer to Him, and to trust in His provision every step of the way.
