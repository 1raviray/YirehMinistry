Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Yireh Ministry.

This Privacy Policy explains how Yireh Ministry may collect, use, and share information when you visit our website or use our services.

Yireh Ministry ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy applies to https://yirehministry.com and the forms and services available through our website.

By using our website, you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not submit personal information through our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, location, and the message you submit.
  • Event information: Details you provide when registering for a ministry event.
  • Donation information: Donation amount and transaction or payment status information made available through our payment provider.
  • Submitted content: Audio files, lyrics, descriptions, or other information you submit through anthem or music submission forms.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device information, and server logs that may be collected by our hosting or service providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to questions and contact form submissions.
  • Manage event registrations and related communication.
  • Process and acknowledge donations.
  • Review anthem, music, or other content submissions.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our website.
  • Protect our website and prevent misuse or fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Donations and Payment Processing

Donations made through our website may be processed by Razorpay or another payment service displayed at checkout. Your payment is subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Payment providers may collect and process payment details directly. We may receive transaction details such as the donation amount, payment status, and transaction reference. We do not intend to collect or store your full card number, card security code, or UPI PIN on our website.

Please review the payment provider's privacy policy before completing a transaction.

4. Email and Communications

Information submitted through our forms may be sent to Yireh Ministry's designated email account so that we can respond to your enquiry, manage registrations, or review submissions.

Please do not include passwords, banking credentials, or other highly sensitive information in website forms.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may rely on third-party services, including:

  • Netlify for website hosting.
  • Render for backend services, where applicable.
  • Google services for email delivery, where applicable.
  • Razorpay for payment processing.

These providers may process information as needed to deliver their services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help operate our website, when required by law, to protect our rights, or when necessary to address fraud, security, or safety issues.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining records, and meeting legal obligations.

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. However, no website, email transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission where required. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review their policies separately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date where appropriate.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact Yireh Ministry:
